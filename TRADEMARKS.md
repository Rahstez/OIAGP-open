Fair, descriptive, and nominative references are permitted to the extent allowed by applicable law. ## Take Gate

**Take Gate** identifies the OIAGP governance control used to evaluate whether required governance conditions remain valid when an agent enters or re-enters a governed role or execution environment. Publication of the reference implementation under Apache License 2.0 grants the license rights applicable to that code; it does not assign ownership of the Take Gate name or grant trademark, certification, endorsement, or background-technology rights.
