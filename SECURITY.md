# Security Policy

## Status

OIAGP is a candidate specification and conformance baseline. It is not a security certification or production guarantee.

## Reporting

Do not disclose suspected vulnerabilities through public issues. Until the publisher establishes a dedicated private reporting channel, retain the report and contact the repository owner privately through GitHub's security-advisory mechanism when available.

Include affected version, threat, reproduction conditions, expected impact, and any evidence that can be shared lawfully. Never include credentials, personal data, production records, or third-party confidential material.

## In scope

Authority bypass, tenant-boundary failure, delegation expansion, forged approvals or receipts, replay, revocation failure, interruption failure, parser ambiguity, unsafe adapters, information leakage, cryptographic misuse, and supply-chain compromise are in scope.

No safe-harbor promise is made by this candidate policy. Final disclosure terms require publisher and counsel approval.
