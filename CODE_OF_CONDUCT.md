# Code of Conduct

Participants must communicate professionally, evaluate proposals on evidence, respect lawful intellectual-property boundaries, and avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, retaliation, doxxing, credential exposure, or unauthorized disclosure.

Security reports and ownership disputes must use controlled private channels designated by the publisher. Public issues must not contain secrets, personal data, confidential records, exploit details under embargo, or allegations presented as adjudicated facts.

Maintainers may restrict participation, remove content, or pause a contribution to protect safety, clean-room integrity, legal compliance, and project continuity. Enforcement decisions should be documented, proportionate, and appealable under project governance.
